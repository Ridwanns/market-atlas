A business thesis needs a price check

Current valuations and 5/10-year earnings scenarios have not been assessed for this basket. Build a conservative case for growth, margins, dilution and the final valuation multiple before buying. A lower stock price alone is not a reason to add.

Concentration means a shared downside

Stress-test a 50% sleeve loss. This is a planning scenario, not a prediction. Keep near-term spending and emergency funds separate. Review customer demand, cash flow, competition and your thesis each quarter.

Research and live quotes are separate

Refreshed quant models use completed daily prices through 30 September; retained financials and options have their own older dates. Live quotes do not refit models.
